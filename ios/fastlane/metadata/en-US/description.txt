Your whole year in one picture. One question every morning, one colour per day – and after a few weeks you see what really shapes your days.

Pixi is a beautiful Year in Pixels mood tracker with a little black cat who asks you how yesterday was. Tap a colour, done. Every day becomes a pixel, every year becomes a glowing picture you actually want to look at.

ONE QUESTION, 15 SECONDS
• A gentle reminder in the morning – Pixi asks about yesterday
• Tap a colour and you're through. No typing, no pressure
• Missed a day? Tap it in the calendar and fill it in later

YOUR YEAR IN PIXELS
• 12 months × 31 days in one glowing grid
• Every map glows in its own colour – mood in violet, sleep in black, crying in light blue, training in green
• Swipe between your maps, tap any day to see or change it

TRACK MORE THAN MOOD
• Ready-made templates: mood, sleep, dreams, energy, period, pain, training, alcohol, meditation, reading, water, screen time, gratitude and more
• Or build your own map: title, question, colours and levels – all yours
• 4 maps free, forever

SEE WHAT MOVES YOU
• Best month, weakest weekday, streaks and distribution
• Put two maps on top of each other: better mood after good nights? Pixi does the math and tells you in one sentence

YOUR YEAR AS A POSTER
• Turn any map into a round year wheel with Pixi in the middle
• Share it as an image – free, no watermark hunt, perfect for your story or as a poster on the wall

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your entries stay on your device. Export them any time

PIXI PREMIUM
Unlimited maps, statistics and correlations. Choose a yearly plan or pay once for lifetime access – no monthly subscription.
