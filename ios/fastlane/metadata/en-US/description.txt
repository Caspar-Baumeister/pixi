Your whole year in one picture. One question every morning, one color per day. After a few weeks you see what really shapes your days.

Pixi is a Year in Pixels mood tracker with a little sketched cat who asks how yesterday was. Tap a color, done. Every day becomes a pixel, every year a glowing picture you love to look at.

ONE QUESTION, 15 SECONDS
• A gentle reminder every morning
• Tap a color. No typing, no pressure
• Missed a day? Fill it in later

TRACK MORE THAN MOOD
• Templates for mood, sleep, dreams, energy, period, pain, training, alcohol, meditation, reading, water, screen time, gratitude and more
• Or create your own map with your own question, colors and levels

SEE WHAT BELONGS TOGETHER
• Pixi shows which colors show up together, like restful nights and radiant days
• Your month compared with the last one, and what stands out on which weekday

YOUR YEAR AS A POSTER
• Every map as a round year wheel, to share or to hang on the wall

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads. Your entries stay on your device

PIXI PREMIUM
Pixi is free with up to 4 maps. Pixi Premium unlocks unlimited maps and all statistics: try the monthly plan free for 3 days, or pay once for lifetime access.
The monthly subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.
