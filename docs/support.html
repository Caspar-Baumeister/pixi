Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Pixi has no account and no server that stores your entries. You can make a copy at any time in Settings with Export data.

How do I restore Premium on a new iPhone?

Open Pixi, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Premium with.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions and Pixi. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your maps stay on your phone either way.

The morning reminder does not show up.

Check that notifications for Pixi are allowed in the iPhone Settings under Notifications, and check the reminder time in Pixi under Settings. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

How do I fill in a day I missed?

Open the map and tap the day in the grid, then pick a color. The check in also lets you switch between yesterday and today.

What does Pixi Premium include?

The whole app: unlimited maps, all templates and custom maps, the morning reminder, the year grid, statistics and connections across your maps, the year wheel and the poster. Start with a 7-day free trial on the monthly plan, or pay once for lifetime access.

Can I create my own map?

Yes. Tap New map, choose Custom, write your own morning question, pick a base color and set your levels.

How do I share or print my year?

Open a map, switch to the round poster view and tap Share. You can save the image, send it or print it.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Pixi is available for iPhone. Android is planned.

Is Pixi a medical app?

No. Pixi is a journal for noticing patterns and does not replace professional help. If you are struggling, please talk to a doctor or a mental health professional.
